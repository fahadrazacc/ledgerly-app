Privacy

Last updated 22 November 2026

The short version

Your financial records are encrypted on your device with a key made from your PIN or password. Copies that leave your device (sync and backups) stay encrypted. Ledgerly shows no ads and does not sell or share your data.

What is stored, and where

  • On your device: your records, accounts, budgets, events and settings, encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
  • If you sign in: your email address (or Google account) is used to sign in through Google Firebase. An encrypted copy of your data is stored in your account so your devices can sync. It can't be read without your PIN.
  • Backups you make: encrypted backup files saved to your phone or your own Google Drive, and CSV exports you choose to create (CSV files are not encrypted).

Analysis

The Analysis screen is worked out on your device from your records. Nothing is sent to any AI or other service.

Your choices

  • Use Ledgerly without an account; nothing then leaves your device unless you back it up.
  • Delete the cloud copy at any time in Profile & settings → Account & sync.
  • Clear or erase data on a device in Profile & settings → Clear data.

Your PIN

Your PIN or password is never sent anywhere or stored as plain text. Because of this, nobody can reset it for you: if it's forgotten, the encrypted data can't be opened.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.
