Budgets that speak up early

Your monthly budget is simply the income you add, so there’s nothing to set up. Add limits for the categories you want to watch, repeating or just for this month. Ledgerly tells you how much you can still spend per day, and alerts you at 80% and when you go over.

Your records are locked before they leave your phone

Your PIN or password creates the key that encrypts everything, on the device itself. What gets saved and synced is scrambled data. The cloud stores it, but can’t read it.

  1. You unlock with your PINor fingerprint / Face ID. The key is made from your PIN and never stored as plain text.
  2. Records are encrypted on the devicewith AES-256-GCM, the same standard banks and messengers use.
  3. Only the locked copy is syncedto your account and your Google Drive backups. Opening it needs your PIN.

Because only you hold the key, nobody can reset it for you. If you forget your PIN, your data can’t be opened, so turn on fingerprint or Face ID and keep a backup.

Questions

Is Ledgerly free?

Yes. There are no ads and no paid tier.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, encrypted with your PIN. If you sign in, an encrypted copy is kept in your account so your other devices can sync. Google Drive backups are also encrypted.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use Ledgerly on one device without signing in. An account is only needed to sync between devices, and you can sign in with email or Google.

What happens if I forget my PIN?

Your data is encrypted with your PIN, so it can’t be opened without it, by us or anyone else. Turn on fingerprint or Face ID so you rarely need to type it, and keep a backup. On the lock screen you can log out and start again, or sign in to another account.

Which currencies does it support?

Ledgerly picks a currency from your phone’s region when you start: $, €, £, Rs, ₹, AED or SAR. You can change it any time in settings.

Why does Android warn me when installing?

Ledgerly is installed from this website rather than the Play Store, so Android asks you to confirm. Allow it once for your browser. A Play Store release is planned.
